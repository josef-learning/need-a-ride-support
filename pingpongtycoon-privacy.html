Privacy Policy

Ping Pong Tycoon: Idle Table Tennis Club Game · Last updated 18 October 2026

Ping Pong Tycoon is made by Josef Larsen ("we") in Denmark. In short: your club stays on your phone, we have no accounts or servers of our own, and the game never tracks you.

What stays on your phone

Your club, your progress, your club's name and colours, and your settings are saved on your device only. We never see them.

Game analytics

To improve the game we receive anonymous gameplay events through TelemetryDeck. For example: which tutorial step was finished, when a club moves to a new venue, how long a session lasted (in whole minutes), which chest was opened, that a rewarded ad was watched, and the product id of an App Store purchase. TelemetryDeck receives a salted, one-way hash instead of a device identifier, and nothing that identifies you. We don't use this data for advertising, and we don't sell or share it.

Advertising

The game shows optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob, only when you tap a button that offers a reward. We request non-personalised ads, and the game never asks for permission to track you, so the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the game or to Google.

To serve and measure ads, Google's SDK collects data such as your approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, ad interactions and diagnostics. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are requested, and you can change it at any time in the game's Settings (the gear at the top of the screen). See Google's privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner apps.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, your scores and achievements are reported to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile, under Apple's Privacy Policy. We don't receive your Apple Account details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the game schedules reminders on your device (for example when your hall is full or the club bus is back). Nothing is sent to us.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because we hold no data that identifies you, we can't look up, export or delete data about a person. Deleting the app deletes everything the game stored on your device. For Google's ad data, use Google's controls linked above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
