Common questions

My purchase didn't show up. Open the Shop tab and tap "Restore purchases". If it still doesn't appear, email us with your Apple receipt.

My progress is gone. Progress syncs through iCloud. Make sure you're signed in to the same iCloud account and open the app; it restores automatically.

Achievements aren't showing in Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app, then open Topspin again.
