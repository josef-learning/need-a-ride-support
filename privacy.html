Privacy Policy

Need a Ride for iPhone and Android · Last updated 10 October 2026

Need a Ride is designed to collect as little as possible. In short: we do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Information we collect

None. Need a Ride has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. We do not operate any server that receives your data. The only outside service the app uses is the map provider described under “Maps” below.

Contacts

The names and phone numbers you add are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Location

When you tap “Pick me up,” the app reads your current location (or a spot you choose on the map) solely to include it in the text message you send. Your location is used on your device to compose that message and is not collected, logged, or transmitted to us. The message — including the location — is sent by you, directly to the recipient you choose: on iPhone through Apple’s Messages, and on Android through your phone’s own messaging app.

Text messages

Messages are sent using your device’s standard messaging (SMS/iMessage on iPhone, your default SMS app on Android). On Android, the app opens your messaging app with the recipient and text filled in, and you press send yourself. We are not a party to those messages and do not have access to them. Carrier message rates may apply.

Maps

The app shows a map and turns the pickup spot into a street address so the message is easier to read.

  • iPhone: the map and address lookup use Apple’s Maps services, handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.
  • Android: the map is shown with Google Maps (the Google Maps SDK for Android), and the address lookup uses Android’s built-in geocoding service, which on most phones is provided by Google. To show the map and look up the address, Google receives the map area you view and the pickup coordinates, along with technical information about your device. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy.

We do not receive any of this information.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. We collect no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
