Privacy Policy

Need a Ride · Last updated 12 July 2026

Need a Ride is designed to collect as little as possible. In short: we do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Information we collect

None. Need a Ride has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

Contacts

The names and phone numbers you add are stored only on your device. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes them.

Location

When you tap “Pick me up,” the app reads your current location (or a spot you choose on the map) solely to include it in the text message you send. Your location is used on your device to compose that message and is not collected, logged, or transmitted to us. The message — including the location — is sent by you through Apple’s Messages, directly to the recipient you choose.

Text messages

Messages are sent using your device’s standard messaging (SMS/iMessage). We are not a party to those messages and do not have access to them. Carrier message rates may apply.

Maps

The app shows a map and can turn coordinates into an address using Apple’s Maps services. This is handled by Apple under Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
