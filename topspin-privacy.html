Privacy Policy

Topspin: Table Tennis Manager · Last updated 23 September 2026

Topspin is designed to collect as little as possible. In short: we do not collect, store, or share any of your personal data.

Information we collect

None. Topspin has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. We do not operate any server that receives your data.

Game progress

Your career (stats, coins, gear, achievements) is stored on your device. If you are signed in to iCloud, a copy is kept in your own iCloud account through Apple's key-value storage so it can follow you to your other devices. We never have access to it.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, achievements and leaderboard scores are reported to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center profile. This is optional and handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Purchases

In-app purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders on your device (for example when your energy is full). No notification data leaves your device.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page.
