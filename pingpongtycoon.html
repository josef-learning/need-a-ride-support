Email josef.larsen.a@hotmail.com and we'll get back to you. Tell us your iPhone model and what happened, and add a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

My purchase didn't show up. Tap the gear at the top of the screen to open Settings, then tap "Restore" under Purchases (the Shop has a "Restore purchases" button too). Passes such as the Owner's Pass, Double Income and Head Coach come back this way. Gold packs and the starter bundles are added the moment you buy them. If something is still missing, email us with your Apple receipt.

Where is my club saved? Your club is saved on your iPhone. If you delete the app, the club is deleted with it. Passes you bought can always be restored on a new phone.

How do I turn off the sound, the music or the vibration? Tap the gear at the top of the screen. Music, sound effects and vibration each have their own switch.

Do I have to watch ads? No. Ads only play when you tap a button that offers a reward, and there are never pop-up or banner ads. In Europe, you can change your ad privacy choice at any time: gear → Privacy choices.

How do I report an ad? In the game, tap the gear at the top → Report an ad, or e-mail us. Tell us what the ad showed and roughly when. We pass it on to Google and block it if we can.

Can I turn off the game's statistics? Yes: gear → Anonymous game stats. The statistics never identify you.

Achievements aren't showing in Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in the iOS Settings app, then open Ping Pong Tycoon again. You can also sign in from the game's Settings.

I don't get reminders. Turn on Notifications in the game's Settings, and check that notifications for Ping Pong Tycoon are allowed in the iOS Settings app.
