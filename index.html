Frequently asked questions

How do I use the app?

Add the person who usually picks you up, then tap “Pick me up.” The app opens a text message to them with your current location and a map link. You can also tap the map to set a different pickup spot before sending.

Does the other person need the app?

No. Need a Ride sends a normal text message, so anyone with a phone can receive it. The map link opens in Apple Maps or Google Maps.

Is my location tracked?

No. Your location is only used to build the message you choose to send. It is never collected, stored on a server, or shared in the background. Your contacts stay on your device.

The app says it’s waiting for GPS.

Make sure Location access is enabled for Need a Ride in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. Give it a few seconds outdoors or near a window to get a fix, then try again.

I can’t send a message.

Sending a text requires a device that can send SMS/iMessage. This won’t work on a Wi-Fi-only iPad. Try again from an iPhone.

Privacy

Need a Ride does not collect any personal data. Contacts and settings are stored only on your device. See our Privacy Policy.
